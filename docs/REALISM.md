# Realism standard

Neon Torino must make it clear **where you are**: anyone who knows Santa Rita should recognise its streets. This
standard applies to Santa Rita and to **every new zone** added to the map. A zone is "done" only when it passes the
whole checklist below.

## Data sources (in order of priority)

| What | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Roads, buildings, areas, trams, shops, trees | **OpenStreetMap** | the base of everything, ODbL licence |
| Missing building heights | **Arpa Piemonte "Edifici 3D 2017"** (`tools/fetch_heights.py`): for Turin it is the real eaves height | connected: 95% of buildings now have a real height. Overture Maps was evaluated and rejected (few heights in Italy) |
| Terrain (hill, Superga, banks of the Po) | **Regione Piemonte** terrain model (5 m from laser survey, downloadable from Geoportale Piemonte) | to connect in M7; Santa Rita is flat |
| Materials (plaster, asphalt, porphyry, stone) | **CC0** textures from ambientCG or Poly Haven | no licence obligation; list them in `docs/CREDITS.md` anyway |
| Difficult monuments (Mole, Superga…) | hand-made model on the OSM footprint; external models only with a verified free licence | credit the author in `docs/CREDITS.md` |
| Colours, signs, details of a street | **Mapillary** photos as visual reference | reference only: the photos never go into the game |

Do not use: Google Earth / Photorealistic 3D Tiles (terms of use), copies of paid maps, models without a clear licence.

## Zone checklist

**Shape**
- [ ] Roads, buildings and areas generated by `tools/build_map.py` (no invented geometry where real data exists).
- [ ] Real heights: levels from OSM; where missing, from Arpa Piemonte (`tools/fetch_heights.py`); invented only as a last resort.
- [ ] The terrain follows the real elevations if the zone is not flat.

**Recognisability**
- [ ] The zone's landmarks (churches, squares, monuments, facilities) modelled on their real shapes in `src/landmarks/`.
- [ ] Real signs of the OSM shops and venues on the ground floor, in their position.
- [ ] Real trees (single OSM trees and tree rows) and tree-lined avenues where they exist.
- [ ] Turin street furniture: GTT stops, street lamps, bins, newsstands, street name plates, parked cars.

**Look**
- [ ] Façades consistent with the period and type of buildings in the zone (e.g. Santa Rita: 1950s–70s apartment
  blocks, continuous balconies, roller shutters, ochre, yellow and grey plaster; centre: 19th-century palazzi and arcades).
- [ ] Materials with photographic textures, light and atmosphere of the high graphics level.

**Play and performance**
- [ ] Runs at 30 fps or more on the low graphics level in phone emulation (`npm test`).
- [ ] No visible drop on PC compared with the existing zones.

**Proof and credits**
- [ ] Comparison pictures of the zone's main spots in `tests/out/` (the test takes them by itself).
- [ ] Sources and licences added to `docs/CREDITS.md`.

## Fixed rules

- **Real shops:** real signs are only there to make the district recognisable. Crime missions (robberies, rackets,
  attacks) only on **invented** businesses, never on a real business with its name.
- **Real people:** no game character carries the name or the look of a real person.
- **Invented details:** when a detail does not come from the data (e.g. the tankers at the Centrale del Latte),
  write it in the comment at the top of the module.
